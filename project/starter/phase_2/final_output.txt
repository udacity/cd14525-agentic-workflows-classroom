=== User stories:
As a Customer Support Representative, I want the Email Router system to automatically categorize incoming messages, so I can focus on addressing complex customer inquiries and issues, leading to improved customer satisfaction and reduced response times.

As an IT Administrator, I need the Email Router system to seamlessly integrate with our existing email infrastructure, ensuring smooth operation and minimal disruption to current workflows, resulting in efficient system configuration and maintenance.

As a Subject Matter Expert (SME), I expect the Email Router system to intelligently route complex communications to me based on content analysis and defined business rules, allowing me to address specialized inquiries effectively and efficiently, leading to improved response accuracy and reduced handling time.

As a Team Leader, I require the Email Router system to provide a management dashboard for monitoring system performance and workflow bottlenecks, enabling me to make data-driven decisions to optimize team efficiency and response metrics.

As a Compliance Officer, I seek the Email Router system to ensure all communications adhere to organizational standards and regulatory requirements, guaranteeing that sensitive information is handled appropriately and in compliance with data protection regulations.

=== Product features:
1. Feature Name: Automatic Message Categorization
   - Description: Automatically categorize incoming messages to help Customer Support Representatives focus on complex inquiries and improve response times.
   - Key Functionality: Automated message categorization based on predefined criteria and machine learning algorithms.
   - User Benefit: Customer Support Representatives can prioritize and respond to complex inquiries more efficiently, leading to improved customer satisfaction.

2. Feature Name: Seamless Integration
   - Description: Seamless integration with existing email infrastructure for IT Administrators to ensure smooth operation and minimal disruption to workflows.
   - Key Functionality: Compatibility with various email servers and systems, easy setup process, and real-time synchronization.
   - User Benefit: IT Administrators can implement the Email Router system without major disruptions, ensuring business continuity and operational efficiency.

3. Feature Name: Intelligent Routing
   - Description: Intelligently route complex communications based on content analysis and business rules for Subject Matter Experts to address specialized inquiries effectively.
   - Key Functionality: Content analysis algorithms, customizable routing rules, and real-time routing updates.
   - User Benefit: Subject Matter Experts can efficiently handle specialized inquiries, leading to quicker resolutions and improved customer service.

4. Feature Name: Management Dashboard
   - Description: Provide a management dashboard for Team Leaders to monitor system performance and workflow bottlenecks for optimizing team efficiency.
   - Key Functionality: Real-time performance metrics, customizable dashboard views, and automated alerts.
   - User Benefit: Team Leaders can track system performance, identify bottlenecks, and make data-driven decisions to enhance team productivity.

5. Feature Name: Compliance and Data Protection
   - Description: Ensure all communications adhere to organizational standards and regulatory requirements for Compliance Officers to handle sensitive information appropriately and comply with data protection regulations.
   - Key Functionality: Data encryption, compliance monitoring tools, and audit trails.
   - User Benefit: Compliance Officers can ensure data security and regulatory compliance, reducing the risk of data breaches and legal issues.
